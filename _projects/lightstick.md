A distributed lighting system for concerts and interactive performances. I designed the **enclosure, circuit, and firmware** on my own: a controller, a gateway, the light sticks, and a web server, all kept in sync over ESP-NOW.

I also designed a **6-hour workshop** in which about 150 camp students built their own light sticks — and then used them in the camp's final performance.
