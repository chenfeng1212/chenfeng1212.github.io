Timing Fixing by Useful Skew

2026 IC/CAD Contest, Taiwan · Problem D · Award · 2026.11

Team of 4 · advised by Prof. Jai-Ming Lin

Our solution to Problem D (proposed by Realtek): fixing timing violations in a chip design by intentionally adjusting when the clock reaches each register — known as useful skew. Our team received an award; the final ranking will be announced in December 2026.

RISC-V Instruction Extension for Post-Quantum Signatures

Undergraduate research project · 2026.06

Team of 4 · advised by Prof. Pei-Yin Chen

SLH-DSA is a hash-based post-quantum signature scheme that spends most of its time computing SHAKE-256. We designed and implemented custom RISC-V instructions for SHAKE-256 to speed it up.
